DriverSpa — cloud CRM for car washes

Run your car wash from a computer or phone, from anywhere in the world. DriverSpa brings together the cash desk, orders, staff payroll, customer base and analytics in one convenient app.

DriverSpa features:

• Cash desk and finances
Track cash and card payments, income and expenses of your car wash in one place.

• Order taking
Create orders in seconds. Services, bay, vehicle and the total are calculated automatically.

• Staff payroll
Automatic calculation of washers' output and wages based on completed orders.

• Deposits, bonuses and discounts
Set up loyalty programs for your customers. Companies can top up deposits and send their vehicles for a wash without extra payments.

• Customers and vehicles
Keep order history, vehicle details and licence plates in a single database.

• Analytics and reports
Get real-time insight into revenue, bay utilisation, popular services and the overall performance of your car wash.
